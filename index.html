Privacy Policy – Tilt Maze

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Tilt Maze (the "App") is developed by Maximasoft ("we"). Contact: erhancppur@gmail.com.

Information we do not collect

The App has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or microphone. Game progress, settings, language and ball choice are stored only on your device.

Motion sensors

The App reads your device's accelerometer/gravity sensor to steer the ball. Sensor data is processed on the device in real time and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver ads, limit frequency, measure performance and prevent fraud, and – where you consent or where permitted – to personalize ads. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent form (Google UMP) and can change their choice in Settings → Ad privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data retention and your rights

We hold no personal data on our servers. Uninstalling the App deletes all locally stored data. For data held by Google as an advertising provider, use Google's privacy controls. You may contact us at erhancppur@gmail.com with any question.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
